# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers must provide a respectful, harassment-free environment. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized behavior, and publishing others' private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or exclude participants for violations. Apply these standards consistently and disclose conflicts of interest.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an available private channel. Do not publish the reporter's or affected person's personal information. The project does not currently provide a staffed moderation service or a guaranteed response timeline.
